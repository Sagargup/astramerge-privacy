1. Information We Do NOT Collect

AstraMerge Pro is designed to function without collecting direct personal identities. Specifically:

  • No Account Registration: You do not need to create an account, provide an email address, password, username, phone number, or social media profile to play.
  • No Contact or Media Access: The app does not request or access your device address book, contacts, camera, microphone, photo gallery, or local documents.
  • No Precise GPS Location: We do not request or collect fine (GPS) location coordinates.
  • No Biometrics: We do not access or collect fingerprint, facial recognition, or biometric data.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To enable gameplay progression and save your achievements, the game stores state locally on your physical device using HTML5 LocalStorage (Key: astramerge_pro_v3_state):

  • Game Progression: Highest level reached (Levels 1 to 50), unlocked chapters, and high scores.
  • Amusement Currencies & Inventory: Virtual coin balance, virtual gem balance, and booster quantities (Undo, Swap, Hammer).
  • Game Settings: Audio mute toggle, haptic vibration toggle, visual theme choice (Cyber Neon, Royal Gold, Emerald Zen), and tutorial completion flag.
  • Entitlement Status: VIP Ad-Removal status (vip: true/false) to suppress banner and interstitial ads.

Local Sandbox Storage: This gameplay data is stored strictly on your local device within the application's isolated sandbox via HTML5 LocalStorage. The game does not implement custom cloud accounts, remote servers, or external cloud-save synchronization. Local data persists on your device until you reset it in the game or remove the application; it is not guaranteed to be preserved across device transfers or application reinstalls.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Technical Analytics (Google AdMob)

AstraMerge Pro is supported through advertising delivered by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). The app offers opt-in rewarded video ads (to earn extra virtual coins or hints), occasional interstitial ads between puzzle levels, and bottom banner ads.

Data Processed by Google AdMob

To deliver ads, limit frequency, prevent fraud, and monitor technical stability, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain technical identifiers and telemetry:

  • Device Advertising Identifiers:
    • Android: Google Advertising ID (GAID), which can be reset or deleted in your Android OS settings.
    • iOS: Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). In compliance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework and Apple Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy), the IDFA is accessed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK only if you explicitly grant tracking permission via the iOS ATT prompt. If you decline permission ("Ask App not to Track"), the iOS operating system restricts access to the IDFA, preventing Google AdMob from using your device's IDFA for cross-app advertising tracking. In such cases, ad requests are handled by Google without cross-app IDFA tracking, and ad delivery is governed by your consent choices made through the User Messaging Platform (UMP) and Google's standard ad serving policies.
  • Coarse Location Data: General geographic information (such as country or region) derived automatically from your IP address for localized ad serving and fraud detection.
  • Diagnostics & Interaction Data: Ad load latency, error logs, ad impressions, click events, and video completion status to evaluate ad delivery reliability and prevent invalid traffic.

For official information on how Google processes and protects advertising data, please consult:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information From Sites or Apps That Use Our Services
  • How Google Retains Data

4. In-App Purchases & Financial Information

AstraMerge Pro offers optional digital items via In-App Purchases, including VIP No-Ads (com.astramerge.neonpuzzle.vip_noads) and virtual currency packs (starter_600, chest_2000, vault_6000).

  • Zero Financial Data Access: All monetary transactions are processed exclusively by Apple Inc. (Apple App Store / StoreKit) or Google LLC (Google Play Billing). The developer never collects, sees, processes, or stores your credit card, debit card, bank account, or billing details.
  • Platform Fulfillment Flow: When you purchase an item, the transaction is processed directly by the native store platform (Apple StoreKit or Google Play Billing). Upon receiving confirmation of transaction approval from the platform billing system via the in-app purchase plugin, the game fulfills the purchased virtual items and sets your VIP ad-free status locally on your device. The developer operates no external receipt validation servers and does not retain customer billing records.

5. User Privacy Controls & Advertising Choices

You have direct control over your privacy and advertising preferences:

A. Apple App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS devices, AstraMerge Pro presents Apple's standard App Tracking Transparency dialog asking for your permission before tracking occurs. You can grant or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings:

Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > AstraMerge

B. Android Advertising ID Controls (Android)

On Android devices, you can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID to restrict cross-app personalized advertising:

Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID)

C. European Economic Area (EEA) & UK Consent (GDPR)

For players in the EEA and UK, we integrate Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to gather consent choices in accordance with the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF). You may select your preferred ad delivery options or adjust vendor preferences when prompted.

D. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have specific statutory rights:

  • Right to Know / Access: The right to request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected, the sources, the business purposes, and the categories of third parties with whom it is shared.
  • Right to Delete: The right to request deletion of personal information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to Correct: The right to request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing: The right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: The right not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising any privacy rights.

Exercising Your Rights: The developer does not sell personal information for monetary payment and maintains no central database of personal player records. To submit a verifiable consumer request to exercise any of your California privacy rights, you may contact the developer at support@repkast.com.

Limiting Cross-Context Behavioral Advertising via Device Settings: Third-party advertising networks (Google AdMob) may collect device identifiers and interaction data that could be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law. In addition to contacting the developer, you can limit or disable cross-app behavioral ad tracking by using your device operating system settings (such as turning off tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or deleting/resetting your Advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads).

E. Ad-Free & Offline Play Options

  • VIP No-Ads In-App Purchase: You can permanently remove banner and interstitial ads by purchasing the "VIP No-Ads" upgrade in the in-game Shop.
  • 100% Offline Gameplay: The puzzle engine is entirely self-contained. You can play all 50 levels in Airplane Mode without an active internet connection.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Practices

A. Local Gameplay Data

Local gameplay data remains on your physical device until you choose to delete it. You can completely erase all local game data at any time through either of the following methods:

  1. In-Game Reset: Open the game, tap the Settings gear (⚙️), and select "Reset All Progress". This immediately purges all high scores, coins, unlocked levels, and saved preferences from local storage.
  2. Device OS Storage Clearing: Navigate to your device settings (Settings > Apps > AstraMerge > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data) or uninstall the application.

B. Third-Party Advertising Data

Identifiers and telemetry collected by Google AdMob are retained and processed by Google in accordance with Google's own Privacy Policy and retention practices. The developer does not control Google's internal retention periods. For official information on how Google retains and manages advertising data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Data Retention Overview.

C. Privacy Inquiries & Deletion Requests

Because the developer does not maintain remote user accounts or backend databases holding your personal records, all personal game progression is under your direct physical control on your device. For any inquiries regarding data protection or deletion assistance, contact support@repkast.com.

7. Children's Privacy

All third-party advertising SDK requests are configured to operate in accordance with applicable child privacy standards.
